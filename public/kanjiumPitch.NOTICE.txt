kanjiumPitch.json is derived from the Kanjium pitch-accent dictionary
(https://github.com/mifunetoshiro/kanjium), as shipped in the Yomitan
"kanjium_pitch_accents" bank. Only the accent-nucleus positions per
expression+reading are kept. It is rebuilt with `npm run build:kanjium-pitch-data`.
